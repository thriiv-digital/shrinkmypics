Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shrink My Pics ("we", "the site") provides a free, browser-based image resizing and conversion tool. This policy explains what data is and isn't collected when you use it.

Your images

Images you resize or convert are processed entirely within your own browser using JavaScript, Canvas, and WebAssembly. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers — we simply don't have a mechanism to receive them. Once you close or refresh the tab, processed images are gone unless you've downloaded them.

Analytics and advertising

We may use third-party services such as Google AdSense to display ads and Google Analytics (or similar) to understand site traffic. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, and may use this data to personalize ads. You can control ad personalization via Google Ads Settings and manage cookies via your browser settings.

Third-party libraries

This tool loads open-source image-compression libraries (libwebp, mozjpeg builds via the jSquash project) from a public CDN (jsDelivr) to improve compression quality. Loading these files may share your IP address and browser information with the CDN provider, consistent with normal web browsing.

Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies to identify you personally. Third-party ad/analytics providers may set their own cookies as described above.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed on the site.

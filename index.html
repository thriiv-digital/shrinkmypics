About Shrink My Pics

Shrink My Pics is a free browser-based tool for resizing and converting images to WebP, JPG, or PNG. Everything runs locally using your browser's own image-processing engine and a WebAssembly build of the same compression libraries (libwebp, mozjpeg) used by tools like Google's Squoosh — so your files never leave your device and there's no upload wait.

FAQ

Is it really free? Yes, no signup, no limits, no watermark.

Do you store my images? No — images are processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded to a server.

Why is my file bigger after converting to PNG? PNG is lossless, so highly detailed photos compress worse than WebP/JPG. Use WebP or JPG for photos, PNG for graphics/screenshots with flat colors or transparency.

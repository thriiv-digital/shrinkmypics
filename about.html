About Shrink My Pics

Shrink My Pics is a free tool for resizing and converting images to WebP, JPG, or PNG, built for anyone who needs to quickly get an image down to size — sellers prepping marketplace listings, bloggers optimizing page speed, or anyone tired of images that are needlessly huge.

Why it's different

Most "free" image tools upload your file to a server, process it, and send it back — which means your images pass through someone else's infrastructure, and the site owner pays for bandwidth on every single image. Shrink My Pics does the resizing and compression entirely in your browser, using the Canvas API and a WebAssembly build of the same compression engines (libwebp, mozjpeg) used by tools like Google's Squoosh. Nothing is ever uploaded.

How it works

  1. Drop in one or more images
  2. Set a max width/height and pick an output format
  3. Download the resized, compressed result — individually or all at once
